Historic Panjab overlay datasets

Cities and rivers: Natural Earth 1:10m populated places and rivers/lake
centerlines. Public domain. Source revision, retrieval date, input URLs and
SHA-256 hashes: app/atlas-overlay-sources.json.
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
These are generalized archival overview data, not municipal boundaries,
current population statistics or surveyed river channels.

Gurdwara location database: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0, with
Wikidata CC0 coordinate contributions; distributed as a combined database
under ODbL 1.0. Retain attribution and comply with ODbL requirements when
redistributing modified databases or produced works.

Identity/history references are factual SGPC, DSGMC and official district /
tourism references. No source photographs or extensive text are reproduced.
Evidence and site-specific coordinate provenance: app/gurdwara-sources.json.
Pins are published representative points, not surveyed entrances.
Featured tiers are editorial. Symbols are original stylized artwork, not
photographs or exact architectural reconstructions.

Pakistan division geometry: derived exact unions of WFP/OCHA 2022 source
district geometry, CC BY-IGO. Source edition and attribution retained in
COUNTRY-DATA-LICENSES.txt and app/neighbour-country-sources.json.
Membership references and supplementary reconciliation evidence are in
app/pakistan-division-sources.json. No claim of current2026 completeness.
