# South Asia Gurdwara Directory — 300 entries

Compiled 8 October 2026. English transliterations; alternate spellings are included in the JSON.

This directory contains 300 distinct named entries across six South Asian countries. The five Takhts, famous 20, and important historic 50 are overlapping selections from the same 300 records. The famous and historic selections are editorial, not official rankings. This is not an exhaustive list of every gurdwara or every historic gurdwara in South Asia.

Locations are at locality or town level, with state/province and country. GPS coordinates, current operation, and access are not verified. A few records are explicitly flagged former heritage sites or associated shrines. Traditional visit accounts should not be treated as independently proven history.
